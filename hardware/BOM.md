## What changed and why

- **Legs: 12 x STS3215 7.4 V (+2 spares), not STS3032.** The old 14 x STS3032 rows were removed (INR 46,586). Reason: Rhoban/bam publishes a measured model for the STS3215 and Open Duck Mini walks on it. Details: docs/research/SERVO_CHOICE.md and docs/research/BOM_CHANGE.md.
- **Servos run straight from the 2S Li-ion pack** (6.4-8.4 V), not from the 5 V regulator. The two 5 V regulators now feed only the Radxa, sensors and audio (one) and the six arm servos (the other).
- **New:** VL53L5CX 8x8 sensor, charging-dock parts, PETG/ASA filament, 15 A fuse, 20 A switch, bulk capacitors.
- **Arms: 6 x GOTECK GS-4308MG** (unchanged). **Brain: Radxa ZERO 3W** (unchanged).

## 3D printed parts

Upload the STL files from cad/out/print/ to robu.in's 3D printing service (https://robu.in/product/3d-printing-service1/, SKU 901845) and set the quantities from cad/out/print_sheet.csv. Choose PETG, and TPU for the foot tips and jaw pads. The CAD is being redesigned for the bigger robot (about 30 cm), so the old file list is stale: do not order prints until the new CAD is done. robu shows the price after the upload; there is no published per-gram rate.

## Not in the list on purpose

- **Servo horns, ear screws and horn screws** come in each servo's box.
- **A bench power supply:** the pack replaces it (see above).
- **A USB data cable for the Radxa's first boot and any 5 V USB-C phone charger:** you own these.
