## Importing and bulk prices (checked 2026-11-24)

- **Importing does not pay.** A private buyer is charged about 31 % of item + shipping (10 % duty on personal imports since the 2026 Budget, the surcharge, 18 % IGST; Mumbai Customs' FAQ) and DHL adds at least INR 1,100 per parcel. Fourteen ST3215 from waveshare.com (USD 16.31 each) land at about INR 2,340 each against INR 2,400 here, with the shipping cost assumed, not quoted; the face panel lands INR 3,000-5,500 dearer than in India; no maker-direct price was found for the STS3032. A Wi-Fi board such as the Radxa can be held for paperwork, and batteries are never imported.
- **No store publishes quantity prices** for these parts (Evelta, Hubtronics, ThinkRobotics, Robocraze, robu were read). Each takes quote requests: ThinkRobotics ('request a quote'), Evelta ('customized quote for bulk orders'), Hubtronics ('Bulk & B2B RFQ'), Robocraze ('up to 30 % off on bulk purchases', no scale given), robu (sales@robu.in). The 12 + 5 servos of order 3 (about INR 45,400) are the row worth a quote.
- **Prices without GST are not a saving** for a private buyer: the tax credit exists only for GST-registered buyers.
- No Black Friday or coupon banner was showing on the stores read.

## What changed and why

- **Price round, 2026-11-24:** every store was read again, with import and bulk routes. INR 5,783 came off by taking the same parts or the same chips from cheaper stores with stock: the Radxa and the IMU from Robodo, INMP441 microphones for the DFRobot ones, a generic MAX98357A and INA219 board, screws in packs of ten with separate nuts, a branded calliper at INR 1,499, Taparia hand tools. Added: a T-plug pair (the pack's plug is a T-plug, not an XT60) and M2.5 brass inserts for the cover's bosses. Dropped: the pendulum weights (the bench fit was skipped). The face panel moved to the one store that has it (INR 67 more).
- **Arms: 6 x Feetech STS3032 bus servos (IDs 13-18), not GOTECK GS-4308MG PWM servos** (owner, 2026-11-21): position and load feedback on every joint, same protocol and driver board as the legs, 4.8-6 V from the 5 V rail B. One STS3032 stalls at 1.2 A, six at 7.2 A: more than the 5 A regulator, so each gets a torque limit of 40 % (about 0.5 A stalled, 2.9 A for all six) that `python -m crab.wiring` enforces. 18 servo cables: three packs.
- **Face: Waveshare 6.25 inch 720 x 1560 colour IPS over micro-HDMI** in a hinged lid with a printed friction hinge, not the 2.42 inch OLED. It takes about 2 W at 5 V from rail A. The panel is bought in order 1 only to weigh it (at most 0.15 kg).
- **Computer: Radxa ZERO 3W plus a home server on Wi-Fi** for heavy work (nothing to buy).
- **Fixes:** INA219 shunt 0.002 ohm (not 0.005); three servo cable packs; a servo hub of about 55 x 36 mm on perfboard; four pogo pins for the dock (Robocraze, INR 94 for five).
- **Legs: 12 x STS3215 7.4 V (+2 spares)**: Rhoban/bam publishes a measured model for the STS3215 and Open Duck Mini walks on it. Details: docs/research/SERVO_CHOICE.md and docs/research/BOM_CHANGE.md.
- **Servos on the pack, regulators for the rest:** the legs run straight from the 2S Li-ion pack (6.4-8.4 V); the two 5 V regulators feed the Radxa, sensors, audio and face panel (A) and the six arm servos (B).
- **New:** VL53L5CX 8x8 sensor, charging-dock parts, PETG/ASA filament, 15 A fuse, 20 A switch, bulk capacitors, the printed body as a priced row (estimate).

## 3D printed parts

The CAD scripts in cad/v2/ write the printable parts to cad/out/v2/print/ (python cad/v2/body.py: the body tray and cover; python cad/v2/coupon.py: a small fit-test coupon to print first). Upload the STL files to robu.in's 3D printing service (https://robu.in/product/3d-printing-service1/, SKU 901845). Choose PETG, and TPU for the foot tips and jaw pads. The full print set is finished with the build, so do not order the whole set before then. robu shows the price after the upload; there is no published per-gram rate.

## Not in the list on purpose

- **Servo horns, ear screws and horn screws** come in each servo's box.
- **A bench power supply:** the pack replaces it (see above).
- **A USB data cable for the Radxa's first boot and any 5 V USB-C phone charger:** you own these.
- **The home server:** an existing PC; nothing to buy.
