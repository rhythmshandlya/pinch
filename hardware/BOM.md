## Why these parts

Every part was in stock on 2026-11-18. The first design's HD-1910 and SCS0009 servos and the Pi Zero 2 W were sold out everywhere, so the crab was redesigned around what was available:

- **Legs: 12 × Feetech STS3032.** Same bus and adapter as before. They report position, load and temperature, so the robot can calibrate and protect itself.
- **Arms: 6 × GOTECK GS-4308MG (5.8 g each).** A big STS3032 in each claw made the arms heavy and overloaded the front thighs. These tiny servos cut the arm weight by four-fifths. Their signal wires go straight to six hardware PWM pins on the Radxa, so no extra board is needed. They can't report their position, so the trim tool (robot/calibrate.py --trim-arms) lines them up by eye.
- **Brain: Radxa ZERO 3W.** The same size and pin header as the Pi Zero, and faster.

## 3D printed parts

Upload the 14 STL files from cad/out/print/ to robu.in's 3D printing service (https://robu.in/product/3d-printing-service1/, SKU 901845) and set the quantities from cad/out/print_sheet.csv (31 pieces, incl. the desk tray). Choose PETG, and TPU for the 4 foot tips and 4 jaw pads. robu shows the price (material, service, shipping and tax) after the upload; there is no published per-gram rate.

## Not in the list on purpose

- **Servo horns, ear screws and horn screws** come in each servo's box.
- **A bench power supply** isn't needed. The first power-up uses the battery, with no servos connected (ASSEMBLY.md stage 2).
- **A 5 V USB-C charger** for the Radxa on the bench (Stage 1): any phone charger with a USB-C cable works; inside the robot it runs from the regulator.
